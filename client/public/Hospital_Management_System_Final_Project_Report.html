Abstract

Modern healthcare ecosystems face substantial administrative bottlenecks due to siloed patient tracking, manual record registers, collision-prone appointment queues, and disconnected billing departments. CareSync Hospital Management System (HMS) is engineered as an enterprise-grade, full-stack digital clinical management solution designed to streamline hospital operations through Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) across four core stakeholders: Hospital Administrators, Medical Consultants (Doctors), Front-Desk Receptionists, and Patients.

Built on the MERN Stack (MongoDB Atlas, Express.js, React 18, Node.js) with Tailwind CSS, the platform delivers high-performance single-page portals for: real-time hospital occupancy tracking, Doctor & Staff administration, Electronic Health Record (EHR) review with allergy telemetry, interactive Electronic Prescriptions (E-Rx) with automated dosage instructions, and itemized multi-tier hospital tax invoicing with audit trails.

Chapter 7: Implementation & Security

7.1 Coding Approach

The platform implements the Model-View-Controller (MVC) architectural pattern on the Node.js/Express backend paired with Component-Driven Architecture (CDA) on the React frontend.

7.2 Security Protocols

  • Bcrypt Password Hashing: High-entropy salting (10 rounds) for zero plaintext exposure.
  • Stateless JWT & RBAC Middleware: Role segregation ensuring patients cannot access doctor or administrative routes.
  • Helmet & Rate Limiting: XSS filtering and 300 req/15min rate throttling per IP to prevent brute-forcing.

7.3 Verification & Validation Results

The system underwent complete End-to-End (E2E) testing across registration, authentication, appointment collisions, dynamic bed allocation, and itemized invoice checkout with 100% test pass rate.
